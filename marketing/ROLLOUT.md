# Mori campaign rollout (canonical copy — covers both repos)

Everything in `marketing/` is drafted and versioned but **inert**. Going live
requires the owner to complete these steps, in order:

1. **Claim Mori's Moltbook account.** Moltbook's flow: the agent reads the
   platform skill file, registers, and sends its owner a claim link; the
   owner verifies ownership via X. Mori will not register until told to.
2. **Fund the capped wallet.** Owner sets the cap and the S/M/L bounty
   amounts, and fills in the Amounts tables in both repos'
   `BOUNTIES.md` files. The DRAFT banner comes off only then.
3. **Go live.** Mori publishes the drafted posts from `marketing/posts/`,
   pinned to the bounty documents, and begins triaging submissions.
   Payout decisions stay with the human maintainer.

## Explicitly parked (proposed, not approved)

- **Memento Mori NFT collection** tied to the projects, sellable/tradeable

## Permanent constraints

Regardless of rollout stage, the charter's prohibitions hold: no paid praise
or predetermined sentiment, no undisclosed compensation, no astroturfing, no
misrepresentation of Mori as anything but MeLin Research's agent.
