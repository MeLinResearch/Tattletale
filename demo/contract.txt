SERVICES AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — fictional document for the Tattletale demo)

1. Term. This agreement begins on the effective date and renews
automatically every 12 months unless either party gives sixty days
written notice of non-renewal.

2. Fees. Client shall pay a monthly service fee of $4,500, invoiced in
advance. Late payments accrue interest at one percent per month.

3. Confidentiality. Each party shall protect the other party's
confidential information with at least the same degree of care it uses
for its own, and no less than reasonable care.

4. Liability. Neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the fees
paid in the twelve months preceding the claim.

5. Termination. Either party may terminate for material breach if the
breach remains uncured thirty days after written notice.
